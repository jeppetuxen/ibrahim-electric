THE BAND

During the last decade IBRAHIM ELECTRIC has been one of the most sought-after European instrumental groups. These 3 personal instrumentalists have - besides the popularity with this group – also made a name for themselves as some of the most striking on their respective instruments in Europe.

Their quirky – provocative – extroverted - original music sticks its fingers into just about every musical cookie jar, and sends cascades of pearls from the speakers.

IBRAHIM ELECTRIC's style is not for the pedantic. There are elements of old fashioned soul/jazz – afrobeat - 60's acid-power-beat - and of course the trio's trademark: the fast, almost-punk passages in which the three musicians merge into one playful organism.

Each of the band-members possess an extremely high level of musicianship plus very large reserves of musical ENERGY! That combination and their mutual love for playing music with each other for almost 20 years now, has made this unit extraordinary!

THE MUSICIANS
NICLAS KNUDSEN
Guitar
Playing professionally since 1992. Has released albums with jazz legends David Liebman, Ray Anderson, John Tchicai, and Adam Nussbaum.

Awards: Danish Grammy (1999), First prize Gexto Jazz Competition Spain (1996, 1999), Roland Musical Award (1998).

Currently plays regularly with bass-icon Matt Garrison and Danish bass-legend Jesper Lundgaard.
JEPPE TUXEN
Hammond B3 Organ
Considered one of the most talented Hammond organ players in Europe – and possibly the most eccentric.

Style compared to Jon Lord (Deep Purple), Booker T. Jones, Jimmy McGriff, and Dr. Lonnie Smith. But he's also a devoted hoarder of vintage synths and retro computers, known to disappear into MiniMoog rabbit holes and coax unholy sounds from Amiga 500s and Commodore 64s. The result? A sonic palette that shouldn't work but absolutely does.

"Jeppe Tuxen treats his Hammond B3 as if both Jimmy Smith and the Devil himself were close on his heels" – Information
STEFAN PASBORG
Drums
Three-time Danish Music Awards winner. Known as a highly personal drummer, composer, and bandleader.

Performed with Marc Ducret, Tomasz Stanko, Miroslav Vitous, Ray Anderson, Ellery Eskelin, and John Tchicai.

Album TriplePoint selected TOP 5 "Best Debut Albums 2007" by All About Jazz, New York.
