THE BAND

During the last decade IBRAHIM ELECTRIC has been one of the most sought-after European instrumental groups. These 3 personal instrumentalists have - besides the popularity with this group – also made a name for themselves as some of the most striking on their respective instruments in Europe.

The Danish trio IBRAHIM ELECTRIC never disappoints. Their quirky – provocative – extroverted - original music sticks its fingers into just about every musical cookie jar, and sends cascades of pearls from the speakers.

IBRAHIM ELECTRIC's style is not for the pedantic. There are elements of old fashioned soul/jazz – afrobeat - 60's acid-power-beat - and of course the trio's trademark: the fast, almost-punk passages in which the three musicians merge into one playful organism.

Each of the band-members possess an extremely high level of musicianship plus very large reserves of musical ENERGY! That combination and their mutual love for playing music with each other for almost 20 years now, has made this unit extraordinary!

Whether Niclas is playing a guitar solo in the spirit of Ali Farka Toure, Jeppe is playing a solo that leads your mind to the Hammond-icons of the 1960's, or Stefan is playing a drum-solo with avantgarde elements, the one thing that overshines it all, is their mutual love for playing and performing, and the sheer intensity of their expression!

THE MUSICIANS
NK

NICLAS KNUDSEN

Guitar

Playing professionally since 1992. Has released albums with jazz legends David Liebman, Ray Anderson, John Tchicai, and Adam Nussbaum. Danish Grammy winner (1999). Won first prize at Gexto Jazz Competition in Spain (1996, 1999). Recipient of the prestigious Roland Musical Award. Currently plays regularly with bass-icon Matt Garrison and Danish bass-legend Jesper Lundgaard.

JT

JEPPE TUXEN

Hammond B3 Organ

Considered one of the most talented Hammond organ players in Europe. Graduated from Rhythmic Music Conservatory in Copenhagen (2003). His style has been compared to Jon Lord (Deep Purple), Booker T. Jones, Jimmy McGriff, Larry Young, and Dr. Lonnie Smith. Critics wrote: "The Hammond-playing by Tuxen has 'God-like' calibre" – Jazz Special Magazine. "Jeppe Tuxen treats his Hammond B3 as if both Jimmy Smith and the Devil himself were close on his heels." – Information

SP

STEFAN PASBORG

Drums

Three-time Danish Music Awards winner. Known on the European jazz scene as a highly personal drummer, composer, and bandleader. Has performed with Marc Ducret, Tomasz Stanko, Miroslav Vitous, Ray Anderson, Ellery Eskelin, and John Tchicai. Awards include: Soloist prize at 16th European Jazz Contest (Germany), First prize at 5th European Tournament for Improvised Music (France), Danish Arts Foundation grants (2003-2005). His album TriplePoint was selected into worldwide TOP 5 "Best Debut Albums of 2007" by All About Jazz Magazine, New York.
